FAQs About Working with a Public Relations Agency

What is a public relations agency? 

A public relations agency develops and executes integrated communication strategies designed to protect, strengthen, and develop a client’s brand. Public relations management services include media relations, content creation, crisis management, internal communications, online reputation building, and public outreach. The goal is to shape public perception, maintain positive relationships with key stakeholders, and ensure your brand is consistently represented in the best possible light across all channels. 

How can your company benefit from a public relations campaign? 

Public relations campaigns can benefit your business by improving brand visibility, increasing media coverage, enhancing customer relationships, positioning your company as an industry leader, and engaging employees, stakeholders, and the community. It can also help mitigate negative publicity, manage crises, and ensure consistent messaging across all platforms. 

A strong PR strategy can position your business as an industry leader and foster trust with your target audience. Without PR, companies risk inconsistent messaging, missed opportunities, and reputational setbacks during critical moments.

Getting started with BoardroomPR is simple! Whether you’re looking to improve your company’s media coverage, build brand awareness, or manage a crisis, we have the tools and experience to help you achieve your objectives. 

What is B2B public relations? 

B2B (business-to-business) public relations focuses on building credibility, trust, and visibility among professional audiences such as executives, decision-makers, investors and industry stakeholders. B2B PR emphasizes thought leadership, trade media placements, speaking opportunities, white papers, case studies and executive profiling to position a company as an authority in its sector. At BoardroomPR, B2B PR strategies are designed to support long-term business growth, strengthen industry relationships and influence purchasing decisions through earned media and strategic storytelling. 

What is B2C public relations? 

B2C (business-to-consumer) public relations focuses on reaching and engaging consumers through mainstream media, digital outlets, social platforms and influencer partnerships. B2C PR campaigns aim to increase brand awareness, drive engagement, and build emotional connections with target audiences. This includes lifestyle media outreach, local and national press coverage, product launches, community engagement and experiential events. BoardroomPR creates B2C PR strategies that tell compelling stories, spark interest, and keep brands top-of-mind with consumers. 

What is public affairs? 

Public affairs focuses on managing communications between organizations and public-sector audiences, including government agencies, regulators, elected officials and community leaders. Public affairs strategies are designed to build credibility, navigate regulatory environments, support policy initiatives, and engage communities on issues that impact business operations. At BoardroomPR, public affairs efforts often include stakeholder outreach, community engagement, issue management and strategic messaging to ensure organizations communicate clearly, responsibly and effectively in complex public and regulatory landscapes. 

What is internal PR and why is it important? 

Internal public relations focuses on communicating with employees, leadership and internal stakeholders to ensure alignment, transparency and engagement. Strong internal PR supports company culture, recruitment, retention and productivity while helping teams understand business priorities and changes. BoardroomPR supports internal PR through tools such as internal newsletters, CEO fireside chats, leadership videos, departmental updates and internal crisis communications. These initiatives give employees a voice, highlight key management perspectives and foster trust across the organization. Effective internal PR ensures employees are informed, engaged and aligned, making them powerful ambassadors for the brand. 

What is external PR and why is it important? 

External public relations focuses on how a company communicates with audiences outside the organization, including customers, media, investors, communities, government agencies and industry stakeholders. External PR builds brand awareness, manages reputation, supports growth initiatives and strengthens relationships with key audiences. Through media relations, trade publication coverage, thought leadership, events, community outreach and crisis management, BoardroomPR ensures your brand’s message is consistent, credible and impactful. External PR is essential for shaping public perception, building trust, and protecting your brand in both opportunity-driven and high-stakes situations. 

How does public relations integrate with social media and video campaigns? 

Public relations works hand-in-hand with social media and video campaigns to amplify earned media, reinforce messaging, and extend the reach of PR campaigns. Media coverage can be repurposed across social platforms, while video content, such as executive interviews, behind-the-scenes footage, event highlights and thought leadership pieces, adds authenticity and engagement. At BoardroomPR, we integrate PR, social media and video to create cohesive, multi-channel campaigns that tell a consistent story. This approach strengthens visibility, drives engagement, and ensures your message reaches audiences wherever they consume content, online, on social platforms and across traditional media. 

Why are trade journals important in a public relations strategy? 

Trade journals play a critical role in building credibility and authority within your industry by positioning your company and leadership team as subject-matter experts. Coverage in respected trade publications increases visibility among decision-makers, influencers and referral sources who actively follow industry news and trends. Trade media placements support both B2B and B2C organizations by reinforcing expertise, driving qualified interest and strengthening trust with key stakeholders. At BoardroomPR, we strategically pitch and place stories in top trade journals to ensure your brand is consistently recognized where it matters most.

We leverage social media, search enginge optimization (SEO), video, website content and internal and external communications to ensure your message reaches the right audience on the right platform. We don’t rely on one-size-fits-all approaches; instead, we tailor every strategy to your specific goals, industry, and audience. What truly sets us apart is our entrepreneurial, opportunistic mindset. We think like business owners, not just consultants, constantly identifying timely opportunities, emerging trends and news hooks that can elevate your brand. Our team moves quickly, adapts strategies in real time, and capitalizes on moments that create meaningful visibility. Combined with deep expertise in media relations, crisis management and content marketing, this approach ensures your brand is amplified strategically and effectively across all channels.

What We Do for You with Our PR Management Services 

Press Releases: 

Press releases are an effective tool to communicate important messages from your company. Content could encompass news regarding expansion, new product or service offerings, emerging trends, lawsuits, recently concluded transactions, noteworthy new hires or awards, company milestones and other honors. These are all positive, important things you want your customers, prospects and perhaps the public at large to know about. BoardroomPR has a staff of seasoned writers, including many former journalists, who know how to craft compelling narratives that will get reporters’ and editors’ attention. Through our personal media contacts, we will create a customized distribution list of reporters and editors that will be most interested in what you have to say. Once we send out the announcements, we follow up directly to get your news placed. 

Media Relations and Thought Leadership:  

In addition to drafting compelling press releases, we will work to get your executives and subject-matter experts quoted in stories, positioning them as authoritative, knowledgeable and credible sources that align with the company’s core areas of expertise. In many instances, our clients are interviewed on news stories regarding the economy and industry trends. Visibility in major newspapers and on television and podcasts contributes to building brand recognition and credibility for the business and its key leaders. We ensure your message reaches the right audience at the right time. 

Byline Articles and Op-Eds: 

Byline articles and opinion pieces offer valuable opportunities for executives and professionals to be published in publications, trade journals and newsletters, establishing them as thought leaders in their respective fields. BoardroomPR publicists are adept at conceptualizing timely and informative guest column and opinion piece ideas and placing them in strategic publications. We work closely with you and the editors to ensure the articles are well-conceived and written with the right audience in mind. Published articles not only serve as additional marketing materials in reprints and handouts but are also effective tools to enhance your website, blog and social media presence. 

Media Training: 

Once it is determined who will become the company’s spokesperson(s), we will conduct a media training session to cover the fundamentals of interviews. Our media relations professionals will also prepare you and your employees for each interview as they come up. We do the advance work with reporters to minimize the possibility of any unforeseen surprises. BoardroomPR offers advanced crisis management and communications planning, as well as on-camera training for your company’s spokespersons. 

Editorial Calendar Monitoring: 

In addition to our frequent contact with the media, BoardroomPR identifies editorial opportunities for its clients by tracking planned stories and special sections on editorial calendars published by business and trade publications. We also have access to databases that deliver story leads daily. Quite frequently, our clients are offered as valuable sources for such stories.
